Mapping Notes

  • FILE is a weak entity set. Its primary key is the primary key of the owner (Repo_ID) combined with its discriminator (Path); the identifying relationship CONTAINS therefore produces no separate relation.
  • The composite attribute Span is replaced by its component attributes Start_Line and End_Line; no column is created for Span itself.
  • The derived attribute Length is not stored. It is computed as End_Line − Start_Line + 1.
  • The multivalued attribute Email becomes the separate relation AUTHOR_EMAIL, whose primary key is all of its attributes.
  • 1 : N relationships (CONTAINS, DECLARES, RECORDED_IN, AUTHORED_BY, GROUPS, REVIEWS) are mapped by posting the primary key of the “1” side into the relation on the “N” side as a foreign key.
  • M : N relationships (CALLS, IMPORTS, MODIFIES, WAIVES) each become a separate relation whose primary key is the union of the participating primary keys, plus any descriptive attributes.
  • CALLS and IMPORTS are recursive; role names are used to make the two foreign keys onto the same relation unique.
  • Total participation is enforced with NOT NULL on the corresponding foreign key — for example COMMIT.Repo_ID, COMMIT.Author_ID and SYMBOL.(Repo_ID, Path).
